Nivra

A private organizer for the browser. Calendar, timetable, tasks and more, stored on your own device.

What is in it

Nivra is organized in four groups.

Main
Dashboard, calendar, timetable
Study
Tasks, exams and projects, grades
Money
Bank, wishlist, subscriptions
Utilities
Notepad, countdowns, reminders

Private by default

There is no sign-up. Nivra asks for your name and keeps everything in your browser.

Works like an app

It ships a web app manifest and a service worker, so it can be installed from the browser and opened like any other app.
